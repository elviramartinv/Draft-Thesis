\chapter{Additional pre-fit distributions}
\label{app:prefit}
%
This appendix collects the pre-fit distributions that complement those shown in Section \ref{sec:bbtt_controlplots}. They are obtained with the same selection, corrections, and uncertainty band as in that section, for the 2024 data-taking period, and are grouped into the control regions, where the corrections of the two dominant backgrounds are tested, and the categories of the analysis, where the complete prediction is compared with the data before the fit.

\section{Control regions}
\label{app:prefit_cr}
%
This section complements Figures \ref{fig:cr_dy} and \ref{fig:cr_tt} with the remaining observables of the two control regions defined in Section \ref{subsec:bbtt_cr}. They comprise the kinematics of the two leptons and of the two jets of the $H\to b\bar{b}$ candidate, the $b$ tagging and \textsc{HH-BTag} scores of these jets, the variables of the lepton pair, of the $H\to b\bar{b}$ candidate, and of the $HH$ system, $p_T^\text{miss}$, the angular separations within each pair, the scalar sum $H_T$ of the jet $p_T$, the number of $b$-tagged jets, and the variables of the VBF jet pair.

\subsection{Drell--Yan-enriched region}
\label{app:prefit_cr_dy}
%
The distributions of the Drell--Yan-enriched region are shown in the $ee$ channel, which was not used in the derivation of the Drell--Yan correction, in Figures \ref{fig:app_dy_leptons} to \ref{fig:app_dy_vbf}. The observables of Figure \ref{fig:cr_dy} are also shown in Figure \ref{fig:app_dy_mumu} for the $\mu\mu$ channel, in which the correction was derived. The prediction describes the data within the uncertainties in most observables. The largest deviation appears in the $b$ tagging scores of the two jets, where the ratio of data to prediction decreases from about $1.1$ at low scores to about $0.85$ at the highest ones. This is expected, since in 2024 the $b$ tagging discriminant is corrected only through the scale factors of its working points, which fix the efficiency at each threshold but not the shape of the distribution (Section \ref{subsec:bbtt_object_corrections}). The data also fall below the prediction at the largest angular separations between the two leptons.
